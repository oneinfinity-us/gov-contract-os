## 4. Reasoning behind the recommendation, and a pattern worth flagging

As with the Employee Engagement Survey evaluation, this score is driven
mainly by two things: (1) **zero AI/Copilot/Azure relevance** — this is a
legacy PeopleSoft/Oracle ERP staffing IDIQ, the highest-weighted dimension
in the rubric, and (2) **zero credited experience** because
`company/past-performance/` is still empty.

⚠️ **This is now the third opportunity evaluated in this system, and the
second in a row, that scores poorly primarily because it falls outside an
AI/Copilot/Azure/software-development core-fit rubric** — not because the
opportunity itself is necessarily weak. If this company's actual practice
includes IT staff augmentation, ERP consulting, or ties into a broader
"Government Contract Opportunity Copilot" line of business that also does
staffing, both this evaluation and the Employee Engagement Survey one
would likely need to be re-run with very different results. **Recommend
prioritizing filling in `company/capabilities.md` and
`company/past-performance/` now** — without them, this system cannot
reliably distinguish "genuinely poor fit" from "rubric doesn't match this
company's actual line of business," and that ambiguity is compounding
across evaluations.

Positives, independent of the capability-fit question: perfect geographic
fit, a workable proposal window (35 days), and clear subcontracting/teaming
pathways for any position types the firm can't staff directly. Negatives
beyond capability fit: the $3M cyber E&O + Professional Liability
requirement is a real underwriting hurdle for a small/new firm, and the
"no minimum quantity guaranteed" IDIQ structure means the $1.9M ceiling is
not a promise of revenue.
